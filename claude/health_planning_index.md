## Part 7 — Preliminary Plan Overview

> This is the initial framework discussed in the first planning conversation. Each section will be expanded into a full deep-dive in its own dedicated chat.

### Training Structure: 3 Phases

**Phase 1 — Weeks 1 to 4: Build the Base**
- 3 gym days per week: Upper body / Lower body / Full body
- 2 light cardio days per week: brisk walking, interval walking from Week 3
- 2 rest days
- Focus: form, movement patterns, core stability, back safety
- No heavy barbell work, no deadlifts, no loaded spinal flexion
- All machine-based and dumbbell work at light to moderate weight

**Phase 2 — Weeks 5 to 10: Add Load and Intensity**
- 4 gym days per week
- Running intervals replace walking
- Progressive overload introduced: +2.5–5kg every 1–2 weeks
- Barbell squat introduced at light weight with form focus
- Conventional deadlift introduced slowly
- Back should be ready if Phase 1 goes well

**Phase 3 — Weeks 11+: Strength and Endurance**
- Full strength program with consistent progressive overload
- Running sessions for stamina
- More advanced compound lifts
- Full detail to be planned in dedicated Section 1 chat

### Phase 1 Weekly Schedule

| Day | Session |
|-----|---------|
| Monday | Upper body — gym |
| Tuesday | Cardio — brisk walk |
| Wednesday | Lower body — gym |
| Thursday | Rest |
| Friday | Full body — gym |
| Saturday | Cardio — walk or interval walk |
| Sunday | Rest |

### Phase 1 Exercise Overview

**Monday — Upper Body (~45–55 mins)**
- Warm-up: 5 mins (arm circles, shoulder rolls, light treadmill walk)
- Machine chest press: 3 × 10
- Seated cable row: 3 × 10
- Dumbbell shoulder press: 3 × 10
- Lat pulldown (light weight): 3 × 12
- Dumbbell bicep curl: 2 × 12
- Tricep pushdown (cable): 2 × 12
- Dead bug (core): 3 × 8 per side
- Rest: 60–90 seconds between sets
- Weight selection: light to moderate, form is the priority

**Wednesday — Lower Body**
- Warm-up: 5 mins (leg swings, hip circles, slow treadmill walk)
- Leg press (machine): 3 × 12
- Goblet squat (light dumbbell): 3 × 10
- Seated leg curl: 3 × 12
- Leg extension: 3 × 12
- Calf raise (machine or standing): 3 × 15
- Glute bridge (bodyweight): 3 × 15
- Bird-dog (core and back safe): 3 × 8 per side
- Note: No barbell squats or deadlifts in Phase 1

**Friday — Full Body (lighter, compound-focused)**
- Warm-up: 5 mins
- Dumbbell goblet squat: 3 × 10
- Dumbbell Romanian deadlift (light weight): 3 × 10
- Push-ups (knee variation if needed): 3 × max reps
- Dumbbell bent-over row: 3 × 10 per side
- Plank hold: 3 × 20–30 seconds
- Mountain climbers (slow): 2 × 20 total
- Romanian deadlift note: hinge at hips only, stop immediately if any lower back sensation

**Tuesday and Saturday — Cardio**
- Weeks 1–2: 20–30 minute brisk walk
- Weeks 3–4: Interval walking (1 min fast, 2 min easy, repeat)
- 10 minute light stretching after
- Goal: slightly breathless but still able to speak; never gasping

### Nutrition Overview (discussed, full plan needed in Section 3)
- Calorie target: approximately 1700–1900 kcal/day (slight surplus to support muscle gain at low starting weight)
- Protein target: 90–110g/day
- Meal structure: 3 meals + 2 snacks
- Breakfast must be reinstated — skipping is not acceptable for muscle gain and energy
- Sample day structure discussed:
  - 7–8 AM: Soaked almonds + warm water, then 2–3 eggs (scrambled or boiled) + 2 rotis or toast
  - 10–11 AM: 1 fruit (banana or apple — easiest options, no prep required)
  - 1–2 PM: Dal + rice or 2 rotis + sabzi + curd or lassi on the side
  - 4–5 PM: Glass of milk or lassi + 4–5 cashews (pre-workout fuel if gym in evening)
  - 7–9 PM: Paneer dish (sabzi, bhurji, or grilled) + 2 rotis — target 100–150g paneer as the main protein meal
  - Before bed: Warm glass of milk for overnight muscle recovery

### Hydration Overview (discussed, full plan needed in Section 4)
- Target: 2.5–3 litres per day
- Simple system: 1 litre bottle on desk, refill twice throughout the day
- 1 glass immediately on waking before anything else
- 1 glass before every meal
- Sip every 15 minutes during workouts
- Urine colour check: pale yellow means hydrated, dark yellow means behind

### Sleep Overview (discussed, full plan needed in Section 5)
- Current: 6–7 hrs, inconsistently
- Target: 7–8 hrs on a fixed schedule
- Immediate action: establish fixed sleep and wake times
- No screens 30 minutes before bed
