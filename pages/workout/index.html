The Two-Session Rule

Complete all sets and reps with correct form in two consecutive sessions before increasing weight. One good session could be a good day — two confirms it.

Weight Increments

Machines and dumbbells: +5 lbs. Barbell squat and deadlift: +5 lbs per side (10 lbs total) — only when form is absolutely solid.

All-or-Nothing Trap

One missed session is normal. The maximum gap between any two sessions is 5 days. Pre-decide this rule now so you don't think about it when it happens.
